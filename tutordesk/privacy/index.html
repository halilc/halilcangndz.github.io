TutorDesk Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 22, 2026

TutorDesk is designed to keep your teaching records private. Student, group, lesson, attendance, note, and payment information is stored locally on your device.

Data collection

TutorDesk does not collect, sell, rent, or share personal data with the developer or third parties. The app does not use advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

Purchases

Optional Pro purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive or store your payment card details. Apple may process purchase information under its own privacy policy.

Backups and exports

If you create an export or backup, you control where it is saved or shared. TutorDesk does not upload these files to a developer-operated server.

Data deletion

You can remove records inside the app or delete the app to remove its locally stored data. Files you exported separately must be deleted from their saved locations by you.
